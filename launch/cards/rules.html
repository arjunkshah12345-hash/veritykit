Reward

Four rules. That is the product.

01

Gate first.

If it did not run, or it did not use the API, the score is 0.

02

Do not stack judges.

Five “is this pretty?” scores are one score, five times.

03

Prefer pairwise.

A 0–10 compresses. “Which sunset is better?” does not.

04

Short allowlist.

A long API dump makes the model invent methods.
